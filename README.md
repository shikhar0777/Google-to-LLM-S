# Analyzing the Shift in User Search Preference from Traditional Search Engines to Large Language Model (LLM)-Based AI Chatbots: A Survey-Based Study

## Overview

This research paper examines the changing behavior of users in information seeking, focusing on the transition from traditional search engines such as Google to Large Language Model (LLM)-based AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, and DeepSeek. The study uses survey data to analyze user preferences, factors influencing adoption, and the potential future impact of conversational AI on search behavior.

## Authors

**Pratik Pokhrel** – Primary Researcher, Project Creator, and Corresponding Author

**Shikhar Pandey** – Contributing Author

## Abstract

The rapid growth of LLM-based AI chatbots has significantly influenced how users search for and consume information. This study investigates whether users are shifting their preference from traditional search engines to AI-driven conversational systems. Through a survey-based analysis, the research explores user demographics, AI awareness, search preferences, and technology usage patterns. The findings indicate a growing preference for AI chatbots due to their ability to provide direct, personalized, and context-aware responses.

## Research Objectives

* Analyze changes in user search behavior.
* Compare traditional search engines with LLM-based chatbots.
* Identify factors influencing user preference.
* Examine the role of AI familiarity in search tool adoption.
* Discuss implications for future information retrieval systems.

## Methodology

* Survey-based research design.
* Data collected through online questionnaires.
* Participants primarily consisted of young adults familiar with AI tools.
* Statistical and descriptive analysis used to interpret results.

## Key Findings

* Most participants preferred LLM-based chatbots as their first search tool.
* Users valued direct answers, personalization, and efficiency.
* AI familiarity positively correlated with chatbot adoption.
* Traditional search engines remain relevant for specific use cases.
* Increased AI usage is associated with higher screen time and concerns about privacy and security.
